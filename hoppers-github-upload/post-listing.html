What to include

A better listing starts with clear expectations.

1

Hostel basics

Hostel name, location, website/social links, photos, and verification status.

2

Role details

Role title, start month, duration, hours per week, days off, and the tasks workers will actually do.

3

Living setup

Staff housing info, meals, shared spaces, deposits, local transport, and any house rules.

4

Pay or exchange terms

Say clearly whether the role is paid, work exchange, paid trial, or paid plus housing.
