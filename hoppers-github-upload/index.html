Founder story

Built because hostel hiring should be clearer.

I built Hoppers because finding hostel work is messier than it should be. Travelers are messaging random hostels, hostels are sorting through unclear applications, and both sides waste time. Hoppers makes the important details clear upfront: dates, roles, housing, meals, experience, and expectations.

Trust and safety

Know what Hoppers is, and what each side must confirm.

Hoppers is a matching and introduction platform, not the employer. Hostels are responsible for interviews, final decisions, role terms, housing, pay or exchange terms, work eligibility, and local legal compliance. Workers are responsible for confirming their own visa and work eligibility before accepting any role.
