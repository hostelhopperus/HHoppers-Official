Founder story

Built to help travelers find work.

I built Hoppers for people who want to travel, work, and actually experience different parts of the world. Finding seasonal work can be messy, whether it is at a hostel, ski mountain, surf camp, hotel, lodge, or somewhere completely different. Hoppers helps travelers find placements around the world with the important details clear from the start: where you are going, what the role is, when it starts, what is included, and what is expected.

Trust and safety

Know what Hoppers is, and what each side must confirm.

Hoppers is a matching and introduction platform, not the employer. Work providers are responsible for interviews, final decisions, role terms, housing, pay or exchange terms, work eligibility, and local legal compliance. Workers are responsible for confirming their own visa and work eligibility before accepting any role.
