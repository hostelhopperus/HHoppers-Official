Trust and safety

Confirm the practical stuff before saying yes.

Before accepting a placement, confirm visa/work rules, housing conditions, pay or exchange terms, weekly hours, days off, cancellation rules, and who to contact if something changes.

Hoppers does not guarantee employment, visas, housing quality, or payment. Workers and work providers are responsible for confirming legal and practical details before agreeing to a placement.
