Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Information we collect

We collect profile details submitted by workers and hostels, including names, emails, locations, roles, skills, dates, profile descriptions, and admin review notes.

How we use information

We use submitted information to review profiles, contact applicants, manage the pilot, publish approved hostel profiles, and prepare future billing setup after approval.

Payments

In pre-launch mode, Hoppers does not collect card information. Future payment processing should happen through a secure third-party provider such as Stripe.
