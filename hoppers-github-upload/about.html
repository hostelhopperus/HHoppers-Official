About

Hoppers helps travelers find seasonal work with the important details clear upfront.

The platform is designed for short-term and seasonal placements, reviewed profiles, and a cleaner introduction process between traveling workers and places hiring help around the world.

For workers

Workers can create a profile with skills, languages, availability, and placement preferences. The goal is to help reliable travelers carry reputation from one placement to the next.

For work providers

Hostels, camps, lodges, hotels, resorts, and other seasonal teams can share roles, dates, benefits, and work expectations so introductions begin with clearer context and less manual screening.

Review-first

Profiles are reviewed before publication. Approved work providers can become visible publicly, and future versions can add messaging, verification steps, payments, and placement history.
