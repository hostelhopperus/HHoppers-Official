About

Hoppers helps hostels and traveling workers build trust before the first shift.

The platform is designed for short-term hostel placements, reviewed profiles, and a cleaner introduction process between hostel teams and people who want to work while traveling.

For workers

Workers can create a profile with skills, languages, availability, and placement preferences. The goal is to help reliable travelers carry reputation from one hostel placement to the next.

For hostels

Hostels can share roles, dates, benefits, and work expectations so introductions begin with clearer context and less manual screening.

Review-first

Profiles are reviewed before publication. Approved hostels can become visible publicly, and future versions can add messaging, verification steps, payments, and placement history.
