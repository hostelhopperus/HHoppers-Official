FAQ

Questions before joining the pilot.

Hoppers is currently set up for reviewed pilot submissions. Payments can be activated after launch.

Trust and safety

What should both sides confirm before a placement?

Before accepting a placement, confirm visa/work rules, housing conditions, pay or exchange terms, weekly hours, days off, cancellation rules, and who to contact if something changes.

Hoppers does not guarantee employment, visas, housing quality, or payment. Workers and hostels are responsible for confirming legal and practical details before agreeing to a placement.

Do I pay when I submit a profile?

No. Workers can create a free profile during the pilot. Hostels can submit a listing for free during the pilot.

Are workers guaranteed a placement?

No. Hoppers helps with reviewed profiles and introductions, but placements depend on hostel needs, timing, eligibility, and mutual agreement.

Are hostels guaranteed workers?

No. Hostels can submit roles and receive reviewed candidate introductions, but matches depend on availability and fit.

Who handles visas and work authorization?

Workers and hostels are responsible for following local laws, work authorization requirements, visas, taxes, and employment rules.

How are profiles reviewed?

The pilot review process checks profile completeness, role clarity, contact details, availability, and basic trust signals before approval.
